Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026. This page explains what information this website collects through its contact form and how that information is used.

Tooele Concrete Pros is a free lead-referral and information service, not a licensed contractor, and does not perform concrete work. Contact form submissions and phone inquiries may be shared with independent, licensed local concrete contractors who can follow up on your request.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the name, phone number, email (if provided), project type and any details you enter into the message field. No payment information is collected on this site.

How it's used

Form submissions are used to respond to your request for a concrete estimate and to connect you with an independent, licensed local concrete contractor. Information is not sold to third parties. Standard website analytics may record general, non-identifying visit data such as pages viewed and approximate location by region.

Form processing

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. Submitting the form sends your information to Formspree's servers for delivery to us. Formspree's own privacy practices govern that processing; see their privacy policy for details.

Phone calls

Calls to the phone number listed on this site may be recorded or tracked for quality and marketing purposes.

Your choices

  • You can request that we delete information submitted through the contact form by calling the number listed on this site.
  • You are not required to submit any information to browse this website.

Questions about this policy can be directed to (385) 469-5163.
