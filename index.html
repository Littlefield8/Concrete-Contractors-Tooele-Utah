Good questions

Tooele concrete, answered

Why does concrete crack so badly during Tooele winters?

Tooele Valley swings from sub-freezing winter nights to dry 90-degree summers. The fix is in the build: compacted base, air-entrained mix, correct thickness and control joints.

How thick should a concrete driveway be around Tooele?

Usually four inches over compacted gravel; five or six with reinforcement for RVs and heavy trucks.
